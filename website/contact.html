[ Contact Information For The Modern Theora Enthusiast ]

There are several ways to obtain more information on Theora.

Mailing lists

All of the Xiph.org Foundation mailing lists (and archives) are accessible here, but you can find the Theora-specific ones here. This is the place to ask if you have specific questions or want to reach the developers.

IRC

There is a channel for development and general chat called #theora on irc.freenode.net. There's a pretty good chance that you'll run into a Theora developer on that channel. Please observe proper etiquette on the IRC channel.

You can access IRC with programs like mIRC for Windows, X-Chat for UNIX/Linux, or IRCle for Macintosh.
