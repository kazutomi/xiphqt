The Speex Development List

The speex-dev@xiph.org mailing list is intended for Speex developers to coordinate development of the Speex specification and software libraries. Please restrict discussion on the speex-dev lists to those topics.

To Subscribe

Send a message containing only “subscribe” in the body to speex-dev-request@xiph.org. Do not send subscription requests directly to the main list; they will be silently ignored.

The list is archived along with the rest of the xiph.org mailing list archives.

E-mail

Have a question about this website? Send an e-mail to webmaster@speex.org.
