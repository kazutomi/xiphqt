Xiph.org QA

The Xiph.org Foundation produces open formats; applications that use these formats are another matter. Depending on what your application does (play audio, rip CDs), there are a number of steps that you can take to keep your app from complicating the lives of your users. This corner of Xiph.org is dedicated to guiding application authors to create better software that uses open multimedia formats.

This page is loosely modeled after the W3C QA section of their site. Functionally, this section of Xiph.org is a corner for us to share best practices in implementing our codecs into your applications.

Issues for Encoding Tools

Encoders are often the first experience a new user can have using Ogg Vorbis or FLAC; further, in the case of lossy codecs, bad compression settings can have a needlessly detrimental effect on the music collection generated. As such, we offer these guidelines for ripper authors:

Encourage users to use quality settings instead of bitrate management

If nothing else, a one-line blurb such as “Setting bitrate explicitly may result in lower-quality files” may be sufficient.
