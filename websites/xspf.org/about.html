About Us

Our group did what it did because we wanted to bring playlist formats up to snuff. There was no standards organization and no commercial sponsorship.

We started work in February 2004, achieved rough consensus on version 0 in April 2004, did implementations and fine tuning throughout summer and fall 2004, and declared the tuned version to be version 1 in January 2005.

During the spring of 2005 we settled XSPF into a home at Xiph.org, a non-profit organization of like-minded people working on audio/video formats like Ogg Vorbis, FLAC, and SPEEX.

A key document prior to our starting work was Lucas Gonze's Survey of playlist formats, finalized in November 2003.
