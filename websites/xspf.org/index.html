XSPF Home

XSPF is the XML Shareable Playlist Format, pronounced spiff.

XSPF is a next generation playlist format that:

  • Is open -- the format is not controlled by any one corporation.
  • Is portable -- You should be able to send a playlist without the content to your friends and have it be usable.
  • Doesn't suck -- Most existing playlist formats suck.

XSPF is a work in progress and is not complete. Take a look at our Wiki -- this is our playground for working out the details of this new playlist format. The draft specification outlines how the playlists are going to work and look, and the schema page has a simple schema and a couple of example XSPF files.

We also have a mailing list you're welcome to join. If you have any comments on XSPF, please post them here.
