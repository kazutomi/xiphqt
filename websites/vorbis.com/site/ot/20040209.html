Previous Issues of Ogg Traffic

1   Status Updates

1.1   Monty

In addition to his work on postfish and finishing CBR support in libvorbis, Monty has started work on OggFile. We should be seeing his draft spec for continuous/discontinuous bitstreams in the next week.

1.2   Jean-Marc Valin

Jean-Marc has continued his work on a fixed-point version of Speex. Version 1.1.4 was released on January 20th with fixed-point improvements and SSE functions modified to build on Win32.

1.3   Ed Zaleski

Ed (aka Oddsock) completed a bulk of the work on the Icecast 2 Beta's and getting it out the door for a final release. He also redesigned the Icecast website, ported libshout2 to Win32, and wrote ezstream. More details on these projects can be found in the News section below.

1.4   Karl Heyes

Karl has released a series of IceS2 betas with small bugfixes and documentation updates, committed a number of bugfixes for Icecast 2.0 before and after release, and is currently working on "burst on connect" and additional Ogg codec support for a future Icecast release.

1.5   Ralph Giles

Ralph committed three patches from Alexander Winston for libogg and libvorbis.

1.6   Timothy Terriberry

Timothy committed another bug fix in Theora's dump_video decoder example.

1.7   Andrew Chatham

Andrew added a number of new features to py-ao, py-ogg, and py-vorbis. py-vorbis 1.4 was released with these changes on December 18th, additions to py-ao and py-ogg are available from Xiph CVS.

1.8   Arc Riley

Arc finished an initial py-ogg2, ported Theora to compile with libogg2 if -DLIBOGG2 is set, and has continued work on libwrit and IceShare.

2   News and Recent Developments

2.1   Xiph.org at SXSW

Xiph will have a booth at the South by Southwest Music Festival Thursday March 18th through Saturday March 20th. Laptops at the booth will demo Vorbis, Flac, Speex, Theora, and Icecast.

In addition, SXSW will once again stream over 100 live performances in Ogg Vorbis with Icecast 2 for those who can't attend the festival. Some of the Xiph team will assist in this project where simultaneous streams will be sent from 10 stages through 4 days of the event.

2.2   New Releases

2.2.1   Icecast 2.0

On January 8th, after 3 years of development and 6 weeks of beta testing, Icecast 2.0 was released. New features include support for both MP3 and Ogg Vorbis, a web administration interface, support for listing in stream directories, and a new stream relay system.

The Icecast team has been busy fixing more bugs and adding features for version 2.1, which is expected to include "burst on connect" support to improve prebuffering time. Support for additional Ogg codecs, such as Theora and Speex, is expected for an later release.

2.2.2   IceS 2.0-Beta 4

IceS2, the Ogg Vorbis stream source client for Icecast 2.0, is nearing official release with its fourth beta release out since January 13th. These beta releases have included minor bugfixes and more documentation.

2.2.3   ezstream

ezstream is a simple MP3/Ogg Vorbis playlist-only stream source client for Icecast 2. It uses libshout2 to send files as-is, without transcoding, to an Icecast 2 server where it can serve as a fallback in case a primary stream fails. ezstream version 0.1.1 is available for download from the Icecast website.
