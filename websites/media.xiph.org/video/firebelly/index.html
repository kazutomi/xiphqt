Xiph.org Test Media

Welcome to media.xiph.org, a repository for freely-redistributable test sets. We use these to test our codecs, and hope you will too.

These clips were provided by Kira Schaffer at Firebelly Productions.

Firebelly chain torches [4:3 | 179 frames ]
ppm tar.bz2 640x480 (26 MB) 320x240 (8.9 MB)
DV Quicktime NTSC 720x480 (22 MB)

Flaming torches at dusk. Fast motion, low contrast, and nasty interlacing artifacts. The ppm files have been cropped and rescaled a bit, so if you want to try deinterlacing, you'll want to work with the original dv version.

Source: Kira Schaffer.

Firebelly by moonlight [4:3 | 314 frames]
ppm tar.bz2 640x480 (109 MB) 320x240 (32 MB)

Spitting fire; moonlight on water. Noisy, varying brightness, handheld camera. Interlacing artifacts. Includes a zoom.

Source: Kira Schaffer.
