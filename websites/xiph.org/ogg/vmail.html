Ogg: Mailing Lists September 2, 2002

Tremor Mailing list

The tremor@xiph.org mailing list is intended for developement, discussion and advocacy of the Tremor integer-only Ogg Vorbis audio codec. Please restrict discussion on the Tremor list to those topics.

Assuming that the email preferences in your web browser are set up correctly, the following button sends a subscription request directly to xiph.org. You'll get an email back with the result; the submit button does not take you to another page.

Traditional method: send a message containing only the one-word line 'subscribe' in the body to tremor-request@xiph.org. Do not send subscription requests directly to the main list; they will be silently ignored.

The list is archived on the xiph.org mailing list archive page.
