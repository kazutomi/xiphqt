Our Audio Projects

Xiph.Org is currently developing four audio-related compression projects; our goal is to protect essential tenets of Internet multimedia from corporate hostage-taking; Free Software and Open Source are the net's greatest tools to keep everyone honest. See About Xiphophorus for details.

Ogg Vorbis

Ogg Vorbis is a patent-clear, fully open general purpose audio encoding format standard that rivals or surpasses the 'upcoming' generation of proprietary coders (AAC and TwinVQ, also known as VQF).

libvorbis, a BSD-licensed source implementation of Vorbis as a library is available; See the Ogg Vorbis page for documentation, downloads and distribution terms.

Further, many players support Ogg Vorbis; see vorbis.com for a list of all the players we know about.

Ogg Theora

Ogg Theora is our new Ogg/VP3 integration project partnership with On2 Technologies. See the Theora.org website for more information.

FLAC

FLAC (Free Lossless Audio Codec) maintains a lossless audio compression codec suitable for preserving audio without sacrificing any audio quality.

The FLAC project joined Xiph.org in late January, in 2003.

Speex

the Speex project maintains a patent-clear audio codec designed for compressing speech data.
