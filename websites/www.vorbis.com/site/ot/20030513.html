Previous Issues of Ogg Traffic

1   Status Updates

1.1   Everybody

Many Xiph.org members were present at the May Monthly Meeting. The meeting minutes contain lots of status updates and other useful information. Since it would be redundant to repeat this information here, I recommend you read the meeting minutes to get the full picture.

1.2   Monty

Monty continued his memory reduction work on Tremor. He eliminated the caching of data structures that made seeking easier. This elimination makes seeking slower, but decode speed is not affected. He also fixed a number of bugs that were exposed by the seeking changes.

1.3   Mike Smith

Mike fixed a tiny typo in ogginfo and updated the icecast TODO list.

1.4   Jean-Marc Valin

Jean-Marc has added a denoiser to Speex, and he committed fixes to lots of small bugs that Segher uncovered.

1.5   Segher Boessenkool

Segher is working on an integer-only implementation of Speex. Details will be posted as they become available.

1.6   Mauricio Piacentini

Mauricio, the maintainer of the pre-Theora VP3 codec, has committed several fixes to the QT6 implementation of VP3. He also checked in VC6 project files for the Theora libraries.

1.7   Stan Seibert

In the middle of coding Positron, Stan fixed a small bug (#336 in Bugzilla) in ogg123.

1.8   Ralph Giles

Ralph has committed lots of Theora changes to whip the file format into shape. He split the codebook portion of the header into its own packet, and he added a vorbiscomment-like comment header packet.

2   Recent Developments

2.1   MPlayer Getting Theora Support

In response to the blurb in the previous issue about Theora coming to Xine, I was informed by an alert reader that there is a patch for Theora support in MPlayer. The patch is available in this message on the mplayer-dev-eng list. If you want to try it out, please keep in mind that the bitstream format for Theora is still in flux, so, as always, Your Mileage May Vary.
