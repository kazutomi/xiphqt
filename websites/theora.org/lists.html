[ Theora Mailing Lists ]

There are three mailing lists maintained for the Theora project. This is the best place to ask questions, contact the developers or other users, or otherwise discuss the Theora video codec.

theora@xiph.org [ archive | subscribe ]
The Theora list is for user discussion relating to the Theora project.
theora-dev@xiph.org [ archive | subscribe ]
The Theora-dev list is for discussion and coordination amongst Theora development team members.
theora-codecs@xiph.org [ archive | subscribe ]
The theora-codecs list is for discussion and maintenance of Theora and legacy VP3 codecs in Windows and Quicktime formats.
